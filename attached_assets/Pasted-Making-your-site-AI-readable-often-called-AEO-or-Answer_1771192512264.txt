Making your site **AI-readable** (often called AEO or Answer Engine Optimization) is the 2026 version of SEO. Since you are using **Vite.js** and **Node.js**, you have a high-performance stack, but you need to bridge the gap between "fast for humans" and "scannable for bots."

Most AI crawlers (like GPTBot or ClaudeBot) prefer structured, static data over dynamic, JavaScript-rendered content. Here is how to optimize your stack:

---

## 1. The Technical Foundation (Vite/Node)

By default, Vite often produces a Client-Side Rendered (CSR) app. Many AI crawlers are "lazy" and won't execute your JavaScript, meaning they see an empty `div id="app"`.

* **Move to SSR or SSG:** If your content doesn't change every second, use **Vite SSR** or a framework like **Astro** or **Nuxt/Next** that handles Server-Side Rendering. This ensures the raw HTML contains your text when the AI bot hits your URL.
* **Use `llms.txt`:** This is a new 2025/2026 standard. Create a file at `/public/llms.txt`. It’s like `robots.txt` but formatted specifically for LLMs to understand your site’s purpose and key links in Markdown.
* **Pre-render Critical Routes:** If you stay with CSR, use a plugin like `vite-plugin-prerender` to turn your main pages into static HTML files during build time.

---

## 2. Implement "Semantic" Structure

AI doesn't "look" at your site; it "parses" it. If your code is a mess of `<div>` tags, the AI gets lost.

* **Semantic HTML:** Use `<article>`, `<section>`, `<aside>`, and proper `<h1-h6>` hierarchies. AI models use these to weight the importance of information.
* **JSON-LD (The "Gold Standard"):** Add Schema.org markup. This tells the AI exactly what a piece of data is (e.g., a Price, an FAQ, or an Author).

```html
<script type="application/ld+json">
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "SoftwareApplication",
  "name": "Your App Name",
  "operatingSystem": "Web",
  "applicationCategory": "DeveloperApplication"
}
</script>

```

---

## 3. Configure `robots.txt` for 2026

Standard SEO often blocks "aggressive" bots. For AI readability, you want to explicitly invite the "good" ones while managing your "crawl budget."

Add this to your `public/robots.txt`:

```text
User-agent: GPTBot
Allow: /
User-agent: ChatGPT-User
Allow: /
User-agent: ClaudeBot
Allow: /
User-agent: PerplexityBot
Allow: /
User-agent: Google-Extended
Allow: /

```

---

## 4. Content Design for Extraction

AI models often "chunk" your content into small pieces to fit into their context windows.

* **TL;DRs and Summaries:** Place a concise summary at the top of long pages. AI agents love grabbing these for quick answers.
* **FAQ Sections:** Use a clear Question/Answer format. These are highly "extractable" and often appear as direct citations in tools like Perplexity or SearchGPT.
* **Avoid Text-in-Images:** If your value proposition is inside a hero banner image, the AI won't see it unless it uses Vision (which is expensive and rarer for bulk crawling). Always provide a text fallback.
