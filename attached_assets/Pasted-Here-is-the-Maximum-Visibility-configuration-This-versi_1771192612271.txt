### **The "Tools" Explained**

Since you asked to "add all the tools," here is what those links at the bottom actually do and why you need them in your `public/` folder:

1. **`sitemap.xml`**:
* **Tool For:** Old-school search engines (Google, Bing).
* **Vite Tip:** Use `vite-plugin-sitemap` to generate this automatically during your build.


2. **`llms.txt`**:
* **Tool For:** AI Agents (ChatGPT, Claude).
* **What it is:** A Markdown file that strips away your CSS/JS and just gives the AI raw facts. It usually links to a broader `llms-full.txt`.


3. **`security.txt`**:
* **Tool For:** White-hat hackers and security bots.
* **Standard:** Standardized by RFC 9116. It tells people where to report security vulnerabilities found in your Node.js app.


4. **`humans.txt`**:
* **Tool For:** People.
* **What it is:** A text file listing who built the site, what tech stack you used (Vite/Node), and your contact info.
